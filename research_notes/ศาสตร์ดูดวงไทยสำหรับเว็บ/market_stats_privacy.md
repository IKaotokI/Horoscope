# Thai Mutelu / Fortune-Telling Market: Statistics, User Behavior, Competitors and Privacy (for a Thai multi-system horoscope website)

Research date: 2026-10-11. Label key: [SURVEY] = primary survey with stated sample; [ESTIMATE] = market estimate/forecast; [SOCIAL LISTENING] = share of online mentions, not share of people; [3RD-PARTY TOOL] = traffic-panel estimate.

## 1. Market value of the Thai mutelu / fortune-telling economy

### Takeaway
The most-cited figure is **THB 10–15 billion (1–1.5 หมื่นล้านบาท) for 2024**, from a University of the Thai Chamber of Commerce (UTCC) survey covering fortune-telling, feng shui and amulets. It is an estimate. The registered formal sector is tiny, about THB 181M registered capital across 164 firms. So the market is overwhelmingly informal: individual fortune tellers, social sellers and temples.

### Cited Findings
- [ESTIMATE] Thai mutelu, fortune-telling and feng shui market of "some THB 10-15 billion" (2024, UTCC survey). Cited by Krungsri Research, "Muteluh: Faith in the Market Place" (2024) — [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)
- [ESTIMATE] Same 1–1.5 หมื่นล้านบาท figure (UTCC, 2567/2024; covers หมอดู, ฮวงจุ้ย, เครื่องราง), restated in an article dated 22 Jan 2026 — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/business/marketing/649633)
- Formal sector (TSIC code 96304, belief/astrology businesses): 164 active registered companies with THB 181M registered capital, 72% of them in the Bangkok Metropolitan Region (DBD, as of Nov 2024) — [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)
- Earlier DBD counts: 134 firms / THB 135.89M capital (31 Mar 2024). 154 firms (31 Jul 2024), with 2023 total revenue of about THB 227.89M. 93 firms / THB 102M capital (31 Jan 2023). Reported via search snippets from [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/business/economy/592973) / [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/business/trade-agriculture/605090) and [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/2526370) (exact article pages not individually verified)
- [ESTIMATE, tourism slice] Ministry of Commerce forecast: mutelu merit-making tourism alone could generate up to THB 10.8 billion, about 0.36% of 2019 tourism value — [thailand.go.th](https://www.thailand.go.th/event-detail/001_02_365)
- [ESTIMATE, tourism slice] Naga-related mutelu tourism in the Northeast (Isan) is put at about THB 15 billion (Thairath, ~2024) — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/economics/thailand_econ/2767752)
- [ESTIMATE, global] Global faith-based tourism: about USD 16B in 2024, rising to USD 38.4B by 2034, a 9.1% CAGR (Future Market Insights). Cited by [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)
- A company-claimed figure: Samart Digital says 75% of Thais (about 52M people) believe in and use astrology services. This is a vendor claim with no stated method. Reported by [InfoQuest/search snippet](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=367921) (source attribution from search summary; treat as low reliability)

### Inferences
- Use "1–1.5 หมื่นล้านบาท (UTCC, 2024 estimate)" as the headline figure. Do not add the tourism figures to it, because the scopes overlap.
- Formal revenue (about THB 228M) is roughly 2% of the estimated market. Most money flows through individuals, social commerce and temples. A website competes for attention, and ad or affiliate revenue, rather than for a formal services market.

### Gaps
- No Kasikorn Research, SCB EIC or Krungthai COMPASS standalone mutelu market-size number was found in this pass.
- No breakdown of the THB 10–15B into fortune-telling services versus amulets versus tourism.
- No estimate of the *online* fortune-telling market specifically.

## 2. Surveys: who consults fortune tellers, by generation and gender; popular systems

### Takeaway
Participation is near-universal. Roughly 8 in 10 Thai adults do some mutelu activity, and 88% say they "believe". **Gen Z is the most frequent fortune-telling user:** 42% get a reading at least quarterly, against about 23–25% for Gen X/Y. Women dominate. Fortune-telling (ดูดวง, palm reading, tarot) is the #1 activity in several polls. Lucky colors, lucky numbers and ปีชง follow.

### Cited Findings
**Krungsri Research survey [SURVEY] (n=1,213, Sept–Oct 2024; 2/3 women; 45.8% Gen Y, 37% Gen X)** — [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)
- More than 8 in 10 have done a mutelu activity. By generation: Gen X 90.7%, Gen Y 82.9%, Gen Z 78.5%, Boomers 67.8%.
- **Horoscope or fortune-telling at least quarterly: Gen Z 42.1%, Gen Y 24.7%, Gen X 22.8%, Boomers 13.6%.**
- Mutelu when facing financial problems: Gen Y 59.2%, Gen Z 53.7%, Gen X 43.6%, Boomers 32.2%.
- Spending on services: about a third spend more than THB 1,000/year. 57.6% of Boomers and 47.4% of Gen Z spend less than THB 500.
- Generation definitions: Gen Z 18–27, Gen Y 28–43, Gen X 44–58, Boomers 59+.

**Hakuhodo HILL ASEAN [SURVEY] (n=1,200, ages 20–59, online, Oct 2023)** — [Hakuhodo](https://www.hakuhodo-global.com/news/hill-asean-findings-on-mutelu-marketing-in-thailand.html)
- 88% believe in mutelu. Top wishes: wealth 44%, good luck 17%, health 12%. Perceived benefit: peace of mind 52%.
- The report gives qualitative generational differences only. Gen Z treats mutelu as a "lighthearted trend" and uses it to start conversations, through lucky-color outfits, wallpapers and stickers. Gen Y focuses on finance and career. Gen X follows traditional Thai and Chinese rituals.
- Generational coverage: [Brand Buffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2024/03/hakuhodo-reveals-studies-of-mutelu-among-thai-people-in-x-y-z-generation/)

**AU Poll (Assumption University) "เส้นทางสายมูของ GEN Z" [SURVEY] (n=950 undergraduates nationwide, Nov–Dec 2023; published 19 Jan 2024)** — [InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=367921)
- 73.2% of Gen Z students are สายมู.
- Activities: **ดูดวง/ลายมือ/ไพ่ยิปซี (tarot) 77.4%**, prayer/wishing 72.5%, shrine worship 69.6%, **สีมงคล (lucky colors) 60.3%**, **เลขมงคล (lucky numbers) 46.3%**.
- Purposes: study 73.7%, finance 60.7%, emotional anchor 59.9%, luck 58.3%, love 55.1%.
- **Info source: social media 86.2%**, friends 48.9%, family 28.5%, offline media 19.6%. 37% see mutelu content at least once a week.

**Ban Somdej Poll (บ้านสมเด็จโพลล์) [SURVEY] (n=1,121 Bangkok residents, 6–9 Jan 2026; published 15 Jan 2026)** — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/local/2907967)
- New Year 2569 activities: **ดูดวง/เช็กดวง 47.9%**, worship 41.4%, merit-making 19.8%, **digital amulets 17.5%**, **แก้ชง 16.2%**.
- Prayer topics: finance/career 45.5%, health 23.7%, love 17.8%, study 11.9%.
- Monthly mutelu spend: none 34.6%, under THB 500 39.7%, THB 501–1,000 17.2%, THB 1,001–5,000 7.2%, over THB 5,000 1.3%.
- 65.7% agree that a bad economy pushes people toward the sacred. 44% see mutelu mainly as an emotional anchor.

**Dataxet dxt:360 social listening [SOCIAL LISTENING] (FB, X, YouTube, TikTok, IG, Pantip; 19 Jan–17 Feb 2026)** — [Marketeer](https://marketeeronline.co/archives/456929)
- Lucky items by share of mentions: **lucky-color clothing 38.2%**, Che Kung (Chakgamhiu) windmill 26.3%, **lucky phone numbers (เบอร์มงคล) 18.8%**, lucky stones 6.9%, omamori 3.5%, other (including wallpapers) 6.3%.
- Wishes by share of mentions: finance 36.4%, love 25.3%, luck 17.2%, career 11.7%, health 9.4%.

### Inferences
- The core target is Gen Z and Gen Y women: they consult most often, buy online most, and discover through social media.
- Priority systems for a site, by evidence: (1) general/daily ดูดวง, (2) tarot, (3) สีมงคล by day, (4) numerology / เบอร์มงคล / ทะเบียนรถ, (5) ปีชง / Chinese zodiac (seasonal around Chinese New Year), (6) wallpaper มงคล. Love readings rank #2 or #3 in wishes.
- Willingness to pay for services is low for Gen Z (about half spend under THB 500/year). Free plus ad/affiliate is the realistic model, with micro-payments at most.

### Gaps
- No NIDA Poll, Suan Dusit Poll, Rakuten Insight or YouGov Thailand survey on fortune-telling was found in this pass.
- No gender split for fortune-telling specifically, apart from "two-thirds women" in the Krungsri sample and women being 2.6x more likely to use online ritual services.
- No survey directly ranking Thai astrology (โหราศาสตร์ไทย/ทักษา) against Western zodiac against BaZi.

## 3. Online behavior: search, mobile, LINE, TikTok/YouTube

### Takeaway
Mutelu discovery is social-first (86% of Gen Z get it from social media), and online purchasing is mainstream: about 70% of Gen Z bought a mutelu product or service online. **67.4% are interested in fortune-telling apps and 52% are more interested if AI is involved.** Hard Google Trends and creator follower data were not obtainable in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Bought a mutelu product or service online: Gen Z 70.5%, Gen Y 67.2%, Gen X 59.3%, Boomers 32.2%. Top online buys: bangles and jewelry 22.8%, amulets 21.9%, **mutelu phone wallpaper 21.6%**. 9.1% used an online ritual service, two-thirds of them paying through a banking app. 2% buy weekly. — [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)
- Interest in digital formats: **fortune-telling apps 67.4%**, auspicious NFT images 26.3%, AR/VR worship 22.3%, livestream rituals 17.2%. About a fifth want none of these (35.6% of Boomers). — [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)
- Social listening: mutelu engagement was 31.1% online versus 68.9% offline (Jan–Feb 2026, Chinese New Year period) — [Marketeer/Dataxet](https://marketeeronline.co/archives/456929)
- Gen Z: social media is the info source for 86.2%. 39% entered mutelu after seeing it on social media. — [InfoQuest/AU Poll](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=367921)
- Livestream fortune-telling on TikTok is described as a growing trend, with readers focusing on relationships, money and the future. No Thai follower counts were found. — search summary of [Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/entertain/670332) (not fetched; low confidence)

### Inferences
- Seasonality, inferred from survey timing and news cycles rather than Trends data: peaks around New Year (ดวงปีใหม่, Ban Somdej poll), Chinese New Year (ปีชง, แก้ชง, Che Kung, per the Dataxet window), and probably Songkran (Thai New Year horoscope) and lottery days (1st/16th). The lottery-day peak is **unverified**.
- Mobile-first is essential: social-media discovery, banking-app payment, and wallpaper as a top digital product.

### Gaps
- **Google Trends**: no published seasonality analysis or term ranking was found for "ดูดวง", "ดูดวงความรัก", "ปีชง", "สีมงคล" or "ไพ่ทาโรต์". Pull these directly from trends.google.com (geo=TH, 5-year window) to verify.
- No mobile-versus-desktop split for Thai horoscope sites.
- No user numbers for LINE horoscope services (LINE ดูดวง / LINE OA fortune channels).
- No verified follower counts for Thai TikTok or YouTube fortune-telling channels.

## 4. Competitors: Thai horoscope websites and apps, traffic and monetization

### Takeaway
myhora.com is the clearest pure-play web competitor: about 2.4–3.1M visits/month, 98% from Thailand, 46.6% organic Google, 41.7% direct, and very long sessions (about 12 minutes). That points to a loyal, tool-heavy audience. The AI fortune-telling entrants (U Destiny by Bitkub Moonshot, MUToday) launched in 2024–2025 but have not published user numbers.

### Cited Findings
- [3RD-PARTY TOOL] myhora.com (Semrush, updated 12 Nov 2025): Oct 2025 visits 2.44M (down 20.78% MoM), Sept 3.08M, Aug 2.63M. Average session 12:24. Thailand about 98.1% of traffic. Sources: organic search 46.61%, direct 41.73%. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/myhora.com/overview)
- Semrush lists myhora's closest "competitors" by keyword overlap as itax.in.th and dailynews.co.th (May 2025). This shows overlap with news portals that run horoscope sections, such as Dailynews and PPTV. — [Semrush competitors](https://cw.semrush.com/website/myhora.com/competitors)
- U Destiny: an AI astrology platform by Bitkub Moonshot with Ultimate Destiny, billed as the first AI-astrology platform in Thailand (May 2024). No user figures given. — [Brand Buffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2024/05/u-destiny-ai-astrology-for-thai-mutelu-people/)
- MUToday combines AI with the knowledge of 7 Thai fortune tellers. The developer says AI alone is not enough. — [Brand Inside](https://brandinside.asia/?p=282240)
- Example app "AI ดูดวง - แม่หมอดูดวง" (iOS, Lifestyle category, ML-based analysis). No download counts are public. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6472936192)
- Thairath runs a dedicated horoscope vertical (thairath.co.th/horoscope) that covers the "AI ดูดวง" phenomenon as 24/7 instant comfort — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/horoscope/belief/2941713)
- Mainstream brands monetize mutelu indirectly: telecom "lucky phone numbers" approved by fortune tellers, bank debit cards with Ganesha/Lakshmi, fashion lucky-color charts — [Hakuhodo](https://www.hakuhodo-global.com/news/hill-asean-findings-on-mutelu-marketing-in-thailand.html)

### Inferences
- Common competitor inputs are birth date, often birth time, sometimes name, phone number or car plate for numerology. A no-login, client-side tool can differentiate on privacy.
- Monetization models seen in the market: display ads (portals, myhora), paid readings and live sessions (TikTok/LINE fortune tellers), and digital goods (wallpapers at 21.6% of online purchases, stickers). Affiliate options include lucky-number SIMs and lucky-color products. myhora's high direct-traffic share suggests that daily-use tools (calendars, daily color, ฤกษ์) drive retention.

## 5. Privacy and legal constraints (PDPA 2562, consumer protection, scams)

### Takeaway
Under PDPA (พ.ร.บ.คุ้มครองข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล พ.ศ. 2562), data that identifies a person **directly or indirectly** is personal data. A birth date plus time plus place, especially combined with a name, phone number or plate, is very likely personal data. Readings that reveal religious or philosophical belief may touch §26 sensitive data ("ความเชื่อในลัทธิ ศาสนาหรือปรัชญา"), which requires explicit consent. Fortune-telling itself is legal. Scams that use it are fraud, and authorities actively warn against entering personal data on unknown horoscope sites.

### Cited Findings
- PDPA definition: personal data is information that identifies a person directly or indirectly. It excludes deceased persons and juristic persons. Indirect identifiers count. — [Marketing Oops](https://www.marketingoops.com/?p=254650); [SCB PDPA explainer](https://www.scb.co.th/th/personal-banking/stories/tips-for-you/pdpa-about-us.html)
- §26 sensitive data includes race, ethnicity, political opinion, **belief in cult, religion or philosophy**, sexual behavior, criminal record, health, genetic and biometric data. Collection is prohibited without explicit consent unless an exception applies. — [iLaw](https://www.ilaw.or.th/articles/3630); [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/511921)
- Government PR Department (กรมประชาสัมพันธ์): fortune-telling is personal belief and not illegal, but scammers use fear ("ดวงไม่ดี") to push paid แก้ดวง/สะเดาะเคราะห์ and money transfers. Do not enter personal or ID-card data on unknown horoscope sites. — [PRD](https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/31/iid/514005)
- Krungthai Bank warns that personal data entered on untrustworthy online fortune-telling channels may be used for identity theft. This is reported in search results citing [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/tech/gadget/1124266) / [Dailynews](https://www.dailynews.co.th/articles/4700319/) (exact page not fetched)
- สคบ. (OCPB) accepts online complaints. The complainant must register with full name and ID number and attach evidence. No สคบ. rule specific to fortune-telling was found. — search summary of [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/business/economy/616695)
- AI concern: 12% of respondents are not interested in AI fortune-telling because they worry about inaccurate predictions — [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)

### Inferences
- Best practice for the site: compute charts client-side (in the browser) and store nothing server-side. Require no account. Do not send birth data in URL query strings. If sharing is needed, use client-only fragments (#) or encoded share images. Use analytics without birth data. If any data is ever stored (saved profiles, paid readings), publish a privacy notice (§23), collect consent, and get explicit consent if anything belief-related is stored.
- Trust signals ("we never store your birth date", no payment for แก้ดวง, an entertainment-purpose disclaimer) answer the scam concerns directly, since government and banks warn users about horoscope sites.
- Wording about results should avoid fear-based upsells, which mirror the scam pattern PRD describes. Under general consumer-protection and fraud law (ป.อาญา §341 fraud), guaranteed-outcome claims are risky. *Not verified from a source in this pass.*

### Gaps
- No PDPC (สคส.) guidance that specifically says whether a birth date alone counts as personal data was found. The conclusion relies on the general "indirect identification" definition. Confirm with pdpc.or.th.
- No statute or regulation specific to fortune-telling services was found.
- No scam statistics (case counts or THB losses) specific to fortune-telling were found.

## 6. Trends 2024–2026: AI fortune telling, Gen Z mutelu, soft power / mutelu tourism

### Takeaway
AI is the main 2024–2026 product trend: 52.2% are more interested if AI is involved, and 28.4% of Gen Z are eager to try now. Several AI-plus-human startups have launched. Digital mutelu (digital amulets at 17.5%, wallpapers, NFTs) is mainstream. The government frames mutelu as soft power and tourism, with figures in the THB 10–15B range.

### Cited Findings
- More interested in fortune-telling if AI is involved: 52.2% (Gen Y 55.7%, Gen X 52.0%). Eager to try now: Gen Z 28.4%, Gen Y 26.2%, Gen X 25.7%, Boomers 13.6%. — [Krungsri Research](https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/research-intelligence/Muteluh-2024)
- Digital amulets (วัตถุมงคลดิจิทัล) were a New Year 2026 activity for 17.5% of Bangkok residents — [Thairath/Ban Somdej Poll](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/local/2907967)
- AI fortune-telling products: U Destiny (Bitkub Moonshot, 2024) — [Brand Buffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2024/05/u-destiny-ai-astrology-for-thai-mutelu-people/); MUToday (AI plus 7 fortune tellers) — [Brand Inside](https://brandinside.asia/?p=282240); how-to guides for using ChatGPT for horoscopes from a major bank — [Krungsri Plearn Plearn](https://www.krungsri.com/th/plearn-plearn/innovation/innovation-sub/how-to-use-chatgpt-horoscope)
- Government portal promotes "Mutelu tourism" and Thai amulets as soft power (popular in Taiwan) — [thailand.go.th](https://thailand.go.th/guide-book-detail/the-power-of-thai-mu-te-lu-soft-power-thai-amulets-shine-in-the-magic-economy-popular-in-taiwan); [thailand.go.th tourism](https://www.thailand.go.th/event-detail/001_02_365)
- Economic stress drives mutelu: 65.7% of Bangkok residents agree that a poor economy pushes people toward the sacred (Jan 2026) — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/local/2907967)

### Inferences
- An AI-interpretation layer over deterministic calculations (Thai astrology, numerology, BaZi, tarot draws) fits demand. Positioning it as "calculated by traditional rules, explained by AI" addresses the 12% accuracy concern.
- English-language or foreign-tourist versions (mutelu tourism) are a possible later expansion.

### Gaps
- No Thai market size for AI fortune-telling and no user numbers for any Thai AI horoscope app.
- No 2025–2026 Gen Z-specific survey beyond the AU Poll (2023) and Krungsri (2024).
