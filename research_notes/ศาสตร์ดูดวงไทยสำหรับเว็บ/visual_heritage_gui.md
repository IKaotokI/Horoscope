# Visual Heritage and UI Design Inspiration for a Thai Multi-System Horoscope Website

Scope: authentic motifs, palettes, typography, iconography, open-licensed asset sources, and modern UI patterns for a "spectacular" (ตระการตา) Thai + Chinese + Western horoscope site. Every license claim is cited; anything not verified is put under Gaps.

## 1. Thai traditional astrology imagery: chart format, planetary deities, manuscripts, murals

### Takeaway
The most authentic and data-driven Thai motifs are (a) the 12-compartment ดวงชะตา using Thai numerals ๑–๙/๐ for planets and "ล/ลั" for ลัคนา, and (b) the nine planetary deities (เทวดานพเคราะห์), each with a fixed body color, mount (พาหนะ) and compass direction. Together these give a ready-made icon set and color system. The best freely licensed Thai astrology imagery is in foreign institutional collections (British Library, Wellcome, CrossAsia/CMU), not in a clearly licensed National Library of Thailand portal.

### Cited Findings
**Chart notation (ดวงชะตา)**
- A Thai horoscope chart is divided into 12 compartments, one per ราศี: "ในแผนภูมิดาวจะแบ่งเป็น ๑๒ ช่องแทนราศีทั้ง ๑๒ ราศี". Signs are indexed 0–11 from เมษ (0) to มีน (11). [th.wikipedia: โหราศาสตร์ไทย](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/โหราศาสตร์ไทย)
- ลัคนา is written as "ล, ลั หรือ ส". Planets are written as Thai numerals: ๑ อาทิตย์, ๒ จันทร์, ๓ อังคาร, ๔ พุธ, ๕ พฤหัสบดี, ๖ ศุกร์, ๗ เสาร์, ๘ ราหู, ๙ เกตุ, ๐ มฤตยู (Uranus). [th.wikipedia: โหราศาสตร์ไทย](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/โหราศาสตร์ไทย)
- The Wikipedia article's image filenames refer to a "Thai wheel" (a circular chart). The text itself does not say whether the chart is square or circular. [th.wikipedia: โหราศาสตร์ไทย](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/โหราศาสตร์ไทย)
- Popular Thai press shows that ลัคนา (written "ลั") is found by ผูกดวง from the date, month, year and time of birth. [Matichon ดาวกับดวง](https://www.matichon.co.th/lifestyle/news_624886)

**เทวดานพเคราะห์: number, body color, mount, direction, benefic/malefic** (source: [Thairath, เทวดานพเคราะห์ประจำวันเกิด](https://www.thairath.co.th/horoscope/belief/2581801); Sun and Moon also confirmed by [Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/entertain/957883))

| ๒ | พระจันทร์ | creamy white (ขาวนวล) | horse (อาชา) | E | benefic (ศุภเคราะห์) |

- English Wikipedia's Thai calendar article shows deity-form images labeled Phra Athit, Phra Chan, Phra Angkhan, Phra Phut, Phra Rahu, Phra Phruehat, Phra Suk and Phra Sao. It also shows Buddha images for each day: three options for Monday, and separate day and night images for Wednesday. [Wikipedia: Thai calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_calendar)

**Manuscripts (สมุดข่อย / Phrommachat ตำราพรหมชาติ)**
- British Library **Or 3593** (1885) is a Phrommachat manuscript. Fols. 2–25 cover "animals of the zodiac" with illustrations of lucky and unlucky omens. It belongs to the Central Thai Manuscripts Digitization project, but the catalogue currently says "digital images currently unavailable". [BL catalogue Or 3593](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-001608329)
- British Library **Or 4830** is a 78-folio 19th-century horoscope manual, also marked as digitised but with images unavailable. [BL Or 4830](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-001608331)
- British Library **Add MS 19351** (1760–1853) is in Thai and Burmese. It has zodiac drawings and omens "in central Thai (late Ayutthaya era) manuscript painting style". [BL search archives](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-002754854)
- A page from Or.3593 (f.14) has been republished under credit "British Library, CC BY". [Scroll.in](https://scroll.in/article/827857/scroll_in)
- Wellcome Collection holds 19th-century Thai Phrommachat manuscripts (MS Thai 5, 6, 7), including a "fortune telling/divination manual based on the Chinese zodiac". This is a good bridge motif between the Thai and Chinese systems (the 12 นักษัตร animals). [Wellcome works/ffc7gxkt](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ffc7gxkt); [Wellcome works/n3evjmdy](https://works.wellcomecollection.org/works/n3evjmdy)
- CrossAsia's Digital Library of Northern Thai Manuscripts has Lanna astrology manuscripts as IIIF. Images and metadata come from Chiang Mai University Library with the National Library of Laos, under **CC BY-NC 4.0** (non-commercial only). [CrossAsia IIIF example](https://iiif.crossasia.org/s/lanna/manifests/560456/c/788778)
- DREAMSEA/HMML has an astrology manuscript from Wat Monthian, Nan. No reuse license is stated. [HMML DREAMSEA](https://www.hmmlcloud.org/dreamsea/detail.php?msid=5654)
- The National Library of Thailand is the legal deposit library and sits under the Fine Arts Department (กรมศิลปากร). [Wikipedia: National Library of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Library_of_Thailand) Its physical samut thai and palm-leaf manuscripts can be studied on request; photography is allowed without flash. [SSRU library news](https://library.ssru.ac.th/en/news/view/s09076901)

**Temple murals**
- Wat Suthat's cosmology (Tribhumi / ไตรภูมิ) murals are painted on 8 square pillars in the vihara, not on the walls. [Chula thesis / TCI article](https://so02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/faa/article/view/241295); [Chula IR PDF](https://cuir.car.chula.ac.th/bitstream/123456789/79192/1/6186724735.pdf)
- Wat Suthat's ordination hall murals run from about 1 m above the floor up to the ceiling and are described as the largest mural area in any Thai prayer hall. [Wikipedia: Wat Suthat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wat_Suthat)

### Inferences
- **Iconography system:** use the nine mounts (lion, horse, buffalo, elephant, deer, ox, tiger, garuda, naga) as glyphs or illustrations for planets. The Thai numerals ๑–๙/๐ work as compact chart markers. This is more authentic than reusing Western planet glyphs on the Thai pages.
- **Compass layout:** the deity directions (NE, E, SE, S, W, N, SW, NW, center) match a 3×3 "ทักษา/ภูมิ" grid. An eight-direction ring with เกตุ at the center could be the hero layout for the ทักษา page.
- **Square ดวงชะตา:** render it as an SVG 4×4-perimeter or 3×4 grid with the ลัคนา cell highlighted in gold and planets placed as ๑–๙ numerals. Offer a circular "Thai wheel" view as a toggle. The exact traditional cell arrangement was not verified (see Gaps).
- **Ornament:** a ไตรภูมิ cosmology backdrop (Mount Meru layers) can serve as a parallax hero, using deep red, gold and black, which are mural-typical colors (inference, not sourced).

### Gaps
- No source found on the **canonical cell arrangement of the square Thai chart**: which ราศี goes in which cell, clockwise or counter-clockwise, and where เมษ starts. Confirm with a โหราศาสตร์ textbook (e.g., Thai astrology association material) or by inspecting myhora's charts.
- **Zodiac ceiling or mural at Wat Suthat / Wat Pho / Wat Phra Kaew:** no source confirmed a zodiac (ราศี) mural at these temples. Treat this as unverified.
- **National Library of Thailand / Fine Arts Department digital collection license** (e.g., Vajirayana digital library, e-manuscript portal): no license statement was found. Assume all rights reserved until confirmed with nlt.go.th.
- **ยันต์ and ลายไทย (กนก, ประจำยาม):** not researched with citations. No open-licensed vector source for these patterns was confirmed.
- The **BL Or.3593 "CC BY" credit** comes from a secondary site (Scroll.in). Check the BL's own rights statement before reuse.

## 2. Color symbolism: Thai day colors, deity colors, Chinese five elements

### Takeaway
The Thai weekday colors (red, yellow, pink, green, orange, blue, purple) are well established and give a natural 7-color accent palette. They do not always match the deities' body colors, and that tension should be designed for on purpose. Chinese Wu Xing colors (green, red, yellow, white, black/blue) provide a second, distinct palette.

### Cited Findings
- Weekday colors: Sun red, Mon yellow, Tue pink, Wed green ("light green at night"), Thu orange, Fri blue, Sat purple. Each day is tied to its planet. [Wikipedia: Thai calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_calendar)
- Thairath explains that Sunday is red because the Sun has the power of brightness. [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/horoscope/horoscope-seer/2897785)
- Sources disagree on Monday: Thairath gives yellow, while other articles give light yellow, white or light grey, citing a legend of the Moon wrapped in pale-yellow cloth. Mahathaksa-based sites further split colors by role (ศรี, บริวาร, etc.), e.g., บริวาร = pink or light red. [KTC article](https://www.ktc.co.th/article/knowledge/birthday-auspicious-color-timetable); [TruePlookpanya](https://www.trueplookpanya.com/knowledge/content/86751---); [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/now/content/2391)
- The deity body colors differ from the day colors: e.g., Moon creamy white vs Monday yellow, Mars copper vs Tuesday pink, Saturn black vs Saturday purple. [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/horoscope/belief/2581801)
- Wu Xing colors: Wood = green (spring, east), Fire = red (summer, south), Earth = yellow (center), Metal = white (autumn, west), Water = black (winter, north). Some sources give Water as blue. The Five Sacred Mountains follow the same direction and color scheme. [New World Encyclopedia: Wu Xing](https://www.newworldencyclopedia.org/entry/Wu_Xing)

### Inferences
- **Suggested tokens:** `--day-sun #D7262E`, `--day-mon #F2C230`, `--day-tue #F27FB0`, `--day-wed #2E9E5B`, `--day-thu #F28C28`, `--day-fri #3A8FD9`, `--day-sat #7B4FB3`, plus base tokens (ink black/indigo night sky, gold #C9A227 for ornaments). These hex values are design proposals, not sourced.
- Show the Mahathaksa role colors (ศรี/บริวาร/กาลกิณี) as a secondary "do / avoid" swatch row. Flag กาลกิณี colors clearly because they mean "avoid".
- **Chinese section:** lacquer red + gold base, with the five-element colors only as accents in ปาจื้อ pillar cells (each stem and branch colored by its element).
- Check contrast: yellow, pink and light green fail WCAG contrast on white as text. Use them as fills or borders with dark text.

### Gaps
- No authoritative classical source (ตำรามหาทักษา) was found that explains why each weekday got its color. Popular press differs.
- The exact hex values for traditional colors are not standardized.

## 3. Thai typography

### Takeaway
Google Fonts hosts the Thai National Fonts (revised by Cadson Demak in 2018) plus Cadson Demak's own Thai display faces. Charm, Charmonman and Srisakdi give a manuscript or "royal" feel; Chonburi gives a high-contrast didone display; Sarabun is the safe body text face.

### Cited Findings
- Charmonman and Srisakdi were added to Google Fonts in 2019. They come from Thailand's National Fonts collection, which the government has used as official fonts since 2007. [Google Workspace Updates 2019](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2019/02/expanded-thai-fonts-editors.html?hl=de); [Wikipedia: National Fonts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Fonts)
- Cadson Demak (founded 2002) revised 13 National Fonts typefaces with Google Fonts in 2018. [Wikipedia: National Fonts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Fonts)
- Charm is a handwritten Thai/Latin family by Cadson Demak. [Google Fonts: Charm](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Charm/about)
- Chonburi is a Thai/Latin didone display typeface from Cadson Demak. [Fine Print School](https://www.fineprintschool.com/free-fonts/chonburi)

### Inferences
- **Pairing idea:** Chonburi or Srisakdi for hero titles (e.g., "ดวงชะตา"), Charmonman for deity names and ornaments, Sarabun / Taviraj / Trirong (looped traditional forms) for long readings, and Mitr / K2D (loopless modern) for UI chrome and numbers.
- Thai numerals ๑–๙ need a font that renders them nicely. Check them in the chosen font.
- Self-host subsets (Thai + Latin) to keep payloads small on mobile.

### Gaps
- The **SIL OFL 1.1 license** of each family (Charm, Charmonman, Srisakdi, Chonburi, Mitr, Sarabun, K2D, Taviraj, Trirong) was **not verified** in this session. Google Fonts families are generally OFL (background knowledge), but confirm on each family's "License" tab at fonts.google.com before shipping.
- No source was found for a Unicode font with Thai astrology glyphs. Zodiac glyph fonts for the Western side were not researched.

## 4. Chinese and Western visual heritage, and public-domain/open asset sources

### Takeaway
Western celestial imagery is the easiest to license: Cellarius' *Harmonia Macrocosmica* (1660/61), the 1909 Rider–Waite–Smith art, NASA imagery and ESA Gaia maps. Each carries specific caveats (US Games trademarks, NASA logo/endorsement rules, ESA share-alike). d3-celestial (BSD-3-Clause) renders real star charts. Chinese-specific heritage (bagua, luopan, seal script) was not covered with citations in this session.

### Cited Findings
**Cellarius, Harmonia Macrocosmica**
- University of Utah Marriott Library digitized plates from the 1661 edition. At least one record carries **NoC-US 1.0** (No Copyright – United States); the rights field is blank on others. [Utah record 73623](https://collections.lib.utah.edu/details?id=73623)
- Public Domain Review features the atlas using scans from Stanford University Libraries. It notes that Stanford collections "have different licenses regarding sharing and re-use", and that many images come from the 1708 Valk & Schenk edition. [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/the-celestial-atlas-of-andreas-cellarius-1660)
- Geographicus sells commercial digital scans; these are paid, not free. [Geographicus](https://www.geographicus.com/P/AntiqueMap/typusaspectuum-cellarius-1660_d)

**Rider–Waite–Smith tarot**
- The original 1909 deck is public domain in countries with a life+70 or shorter term, including the UK, since Pamela Colman Smith died in 1951 (UK/EU public domain from 1 Jan 2022). In the US it is public domain because it was published in 1909. [Wikipedia: Rider–Waite Tarot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_Tarot); [Global Spiritual Studies](https://globalspiritualstudies.com/is-rider-waite-smith-public-domain/)
- US Games owns the RIDER-WAITE® trademark and copyright in its 1971 recolored edition. Do not use the "Rider-Waite" name for a product, and scan from the original 1909 cards, not US Games editions. [The Wild Hunt correction](https://wildhunt.org/2021/01/rider-waite-smith-tarot-deck-may-enter-public-domain.html)

**NASA**
- NASA material is "generally not subject to copyright in the United States", with credit to NASA requested. Exceptions: the NASA insignia and logotype are "not in the public domain"; no implied endorsement; no use of the insignia alongside AI-generated imagery; third-party material marked as copyrighted is excluded. [NASA Brand Center: Images and Media](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)

**ESA Gaia**
- Gaia all-sky maps (e.g., "Gaia's sky in colour", the EDR3 colour map) are **CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO**, credited "ESA/Gaia/DPAC". Reuse needs attribution, a license link and a note of changes; adaptations must be share-alike. [ESA: Gaia's sky in colour](https://sci.esa.int/web/gaia/-/60196-gaia-s-sky-in-colour-equirectangular-projection); [ESA: EDR3 colour map](https://sci.esa.int/web/gaia/-/the-colour-of-the-sky-from-gaia-s-early-data-release-3-equirectangular-projection)

**d3-celestial**
- Licensed **BSD-3-Clause** (repo ofrohn/d3-celestial; npm v0.7.35). [jsDelivr d3-celestial](https://www.jsdelivr.com/package/npm/d3-celestial); [Ecosyste.ms](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/2434130)

**Rawpixel**
- Rawpixel's public domain collection is labeled CC0, free for personal and commercial use. Sources conflict on extra conditions: one claims an "add significant artistic value" requirement when monetizing, and one claims attribution is required. Premium Rawpixel images use a separate, restrictive license. [licenseorg guide](https://licenseorg.com/guide/images/rawpixel); [Bizee](https://bizee.com/marketing/guides/best-sites-for-free-images-for-business)

### Inferences
- **Hero background:** Gaia's equirectangular sky map as a WebGL sky-sphere texture is authentic and striking. Credit "ESA/Gaia/DPAC, CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO" in the footer; the texture itself is share-alike, but the site code is not affected.
- **Live star chart of the birth moment:** d3-celestial (BSD) can draw the actual sky at birth date/time/place, which fits the Western section well.
- **Tarot birth card:** use 1909 RWS scans (e.g., the Wikimedia Commons RWS set) and name it "Smith–Waite" or "tarot 1909" rather than "Rider-Waite".
- **Western section:** Cellarius plates as section dividers or a parallax layer. Prefer the Utah NoC-US scans and check each record.

### Gaps
- **Chinese heritage imagery** (bagua, luopan, 12-animal paintings, seal script fonts such as open-licensed 篆书 fonts) is uncited. No licensed sources were verified this session.
- **Uranometria (Bayer 1603)** scans and their licenses were not checked.
- **Wikimedia Commons categories:** none were verified for Thai zodiac manuscripts. Search Commons for "Phrommachat" and "Or. 3593" directly.
- **Thai government open data / heritage archives** (e.g., Fine Arts Department e-archives, data.go.th): license terms not found.

## 5. Modern UI references and web tech for "spectacular" effects

### Takeaway
Co–Star succeeds through restraint (black-and-white, paper-like, text-led). The Pattern is sober and therapy-like, Sanctuary is playful (gifs, emojis), and Nebula is rebranded around "inner universe" empathy. A Thai site can be "ตระการตา" by using one heavy moment (a WebGL starfield or chart reveal) on top of a calm reading layout, and it must honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Cited Findings
- Co–Star uses a black-and-white, minimalist interface without "flashy colors or bold fonts". Co-founder Banu Guler says it "feels like paper". Imagery is eclectic (owls, stones) plus planet graphs; NASA is used only for chart data. [34th Street](https://www.34st.com/article/2019/01/costar-app-astrology-horoscope-aesthetic-minimalism-tech-social-media-banu-guler)
- Co–Star's onboarding is "stark, minimalist" with a poetic intro. [ScreensDesign: Co–Star](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/costar-personalized-astrology)
- The Pattern is sober and clinical, treating the chart "more like a therapy session" with a serious palette. Sanctuary intersperses "jokes, gifs and emojis". [Bustle review](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)
- A Pratt IxD critique credits The Pattern's high-contrast buttons with easy discoverability during onboarding. [Pratt IxD](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2023/02/design-critique-the-pattern/)
- Moving Brand repositioned Nebula from "viewing the universe" to "exploring the inner self". Its research found users want emotional empathy and flexibility rather than fixed answers. [Design Compass: Nebula](https://designcompass.org/en/archive/nebula/)
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` reflects OS settings (Android 9+ "Remove animations", Windows 11 "Animation effects", iOS/macOS "Reduce motion"). It has been Baseline widely available since January 2020. Best practice is to remove or replace motion (e.g., fade instead of scale or parallax), not just slow it down. [MDN: prefers-reduced-motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion)

### Inferences
- **Signature moments:**
  1. A birth-time input that "ignites" a starfield and animates planets into the ดวงชะตา grid (Thai numerals fly into cells; ลัคนา cell glows gold).
  2. A 3D CSS card flip for the tarot birth card.
  3. Rotating SVG rings for the zodiac wheel and the 12 นักษัตร wheel.
  4. A day-color "aura" gradient keyed to the user's birth weekday.
- **Tech:** Three.js or plain WebGL points for the starfield (cap particles at about 2–5k and DPR at 1.5 on mid-range Android). GSAP or CSS for SVG choreography. Lottie only for small loops. SVG for charts so they stay accessible and selectable. Lazy-load WebGL after first paint and fall back to a static Gaia JPEG.
- **Reduced motion:** a static sky, instant chart rendering and crossfades only. Also offer a manual "ปิดเอฟเฟกต์" toggle.
- **Tone mix:** follow Co–Star's restraint for reading text (generous whitespace, one display font), and reserve the ornamental Thai layer (กนก borders, gold, deity illustrations) for headers and frames.

### Gaps
- No data found on **mid-range Android performance in Thailand** (device share, GPU tiers). Use WebPageTest/Lighthouse throttling as a proxy.
- **Thai competitor UI** (myhora.com, Horoworld) was not analyzed with citations this session.
- **CHANI** and **Sanctuary** visual design were only briefly sourced. No Dribbble/Behance case studies were verified.
